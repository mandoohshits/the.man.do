THE.MAN.DO GIVES

No institution. No middleman. Just people helping people.

Why this exists

In this busy life, some people think of helping but can't find the way. We want to make that possible. You have an idea — we act on it. You have something to give — we make sure it reaches a child who needs it.

This is not a charity organisation. This is a community that moves. Every dirham, every idea, every photo of what we did — documented here. No secrets. No overhead. Just the work.

01 — Your idea
GOT AN IDEA?

You don't have to do it yourself. Tell us what you think we can do — food, school supplies, clothes, shelter, anything. If it's real and it helps a child, we'll find a way to make it happen.

02 — Give what you can
SUPPORT THE WORK

Every amount matters. AED 10 can buy a meal. AED 50 can buy school books. AED 200 can change a week for a family. Give what you can — we show you exactly what it became.

Payment handled directly. Every transaction will be photographed and posted to the proof wall below.

03 — Give your time
CAN'T GIVE MONEY? GIVE THIS.

Skills, time, connections — sometimes worth more than money. If you can teach, drive, cook, photograph, build, or just show up — tell us.

We don't ask where you're from.
We don't ask how much you make.
We ask: what do you have today that a child needs?
That's it. That's the whole thing.

No board of directors.
No admin fees. No waiting.
You give on Monday.
We show proof by Friday.
