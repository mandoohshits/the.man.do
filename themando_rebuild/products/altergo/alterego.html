AE
What Makes It Ours

Not a Logo.
A Secret.

Two marks. No logo. Both only found by the person who already belongs to the story.

01
The Collar Word

One word, embroidered on the inside of the collar — hidden until turned up. Every design carries its own word, tied to its story. The piece built around losing someone might carry STILL HERE.

Not meant to be seen. Meant to be found.
02
The Signature Thread

A single hand-tied knot of raw, undyed thread at the inside side-seam, near the hem. Unbranded, almost invisible. Felt more than seen. It marks every piece as Alterego without ever saying the name.

The quiet thread that holds the story together.
